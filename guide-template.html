Records Management Module — Complete User Guide

A detailed, step-by-step text guide for Third Level Personnel to complete, manage, verify, and export official records.

Notice for Senior Personnel & Applicants: This guide provides plain-language instructions for every screen, form field, dropdown option, document upload, and button in the Records Management System. Please follow each step carefully.
A

System Access & Workspace Layout

Step 1 — Accessing the Records Management System

To begin, log in to your account to open your Records Management Workspace.

System Access & Workspace Layout — Video Walkthrough
What You See

The Sign In page displaying input fields for Email Address and Password, alongside a button for Passcode Login (PIN).

What To Do
  • Enter your official registered DepEd Email Address in the email field.
  • Enter your password, or click Passcode Login to use your 6-digit Security PIN.
  • Click the Sign In button.
What You Should See Next

The system will authenticate your account and automatically open the Records Management Workspace.

Important Note

If you are a first-time applicant whose profile has not yet been initialized, the screen will display a message stating "Record Not Found" along with an Initialize My Profile button. Click Initialize My Profile to create your official record structure.

Once signed in, review the top header banner and the navigation menu.

Tab 1 — Personal Information

Field-by-Field Instructions

Click 1. Personal Info on the navigation menu. Complete every field described below. All text entries are automatically converted to uppercase characters for official uniformity.

1. CSC Format ID Picture (2x2 Photo) Required for 100% Completeness
What this means
Your official passport-size identification photograph required for Civil Service Commission files.
What to upload
A passport-size photo with a white background, featuring a handwritten name tag and signature.
Accepted Format & Size
`.png` or `.jpg` image files. The system automatically compresses the image client-side to 800px.
How to upload
Click the dashed Upload Photo box, select your photo file from your computer, and wait for the upload to complete.
What happens next
Your uploaded photo instantly displays in the 2x2 box and in the top header banner avatar.
2. First Name Required
What this means
Your legal given name as registered in official birth and appointment documents.
What to enter
Type your first name in the First Name text box (e.g., JUAN).
Format
Text input. Auto-converts to UPPERCASE.
Blank Restriction
Cannot be left blank. Needed to calculate overall completeness score.
3. Last Name Required
What this means
Your official family surname.
What to enter
Type your family name in the Last Name text box (e.g., DELA CRUZ).
Format
Text input. Auto-converts to UPPERCASE.
4. Middle Name Optional
What this means
Your maternal surname or middle name.
What to enter
Type your middle name in the Middle Name text box (e.g., SANTOS). If you do not have a middle name, leave this field blank.
5. Suffix Optional
What this means
Name extension following your surname, such as Jr., Sr., III, IV.
What to enter
Type your name suffix (e.g., JR. or III). If you have no suffix, type "Not Applicable".
6. Gender Required
What this means
Your biological sex classification.
How to select
Click the Gender dropdown box, review the choices, and select `Male` or `Female`.
7. Date of Birth Required
What this means
Your official date of birth used to compute your age.
How to select
Click the Date of Birth field to open the calendar picker. Select your birth year, month, and day.
Format & Restriction
Format: YYYY-MM-DD. Birth date cannot be today or in the future. Selecting a future date triggers a red warning popup: "Date of Birth cannot be in the future."
8. Age Auto-Computed
What this means
Displays your current age in years.
Behavior
This field is read-only. As soon as you select your Date of Birth, the system automatically calculates and displays your age.
9. Civil Status Required
What this means
Your current legal marital status.
How to select
Click the Civil Status dropdown menu and select one of the available choices: `Single`, `Married`, `Widowed`, or `Separated`.
10. Employment Status Optional
What this means
Your current appointment tenure category in government service.
How to select
Click the Employment Status dropdown and select `Regular` or `Coterminous`.
11. Position Title (As per Appointment) Required
What this means
Your official plantilla position title as specified in your appointment document.
How to select
Click the Position Title dropdown menu. Review the options (e.g., Secretary, Undersecretary, Assistant Secretary, Director IV, Director III, Regional Director, Assistant Regional Director, Schools Division Superintendent, Assistant Schools Division Superintendent, Chief Administrative Officer, Supervising Administrative Officer, Administrative Officer V, Education Program Supervisor, Public Schools District Supervisor, Principal IV, Principal III, Principal II, Principal I) and select your position.
Custom Position Rule
If your title is not listed, select `Others`. This reveals a text box titled "Please specify position title". Type your title in the box. Leaving it blank or unselected triggers a validation alert: "Please specify your Position Title."
12. Officer-in-Charge (OIC) Status & Designation Conditional
What this means
Indicates whether you are serving in an Officer-in-Charge capacity for an office beyond your plantilla position.
How to operate
If you are an OIC, click the Officer-in-Charge (OIC) Status toggle switch to turn it ON (switch turns dark blue). This action automatically reveals the Designation dropdown field.
Designation Field Rule
When OIC is ON, you MUST select a Designation from the dropdown list or select `Others` and specify it. Leaving it blank triggers an error popup: "Please specify your Designation." If OIC is OFF, the Designation field is hidden.
13. Date of Present Position (Appointment Date) Required
What this means
The effective date of your current position appointment.
How to select
Click the date picker field and select your appointment date (Format: YYYY-MM-DD).
14. Permanent & Temporary Addresses Permanent Address Required
What this means
Your official legal residence and current temporary living address.
What to enter
Type your complete house number, street, barangay, city/municipality, and province in the Permanent Address text box (e.g., 123 MAIN ST., BRGY. CENTRAL, QUEZON CITY, METRO MANILA). Enter your Temporary Address if different.
15. Phone Number & Alternative Email Addresses Optional
Phone Number
Type your 11-digit mobile contact number (digits only, e.g., 09123456789).
Alternative Email 1 & 2
Enter backup email addresses (e.g., personal@gmail.com). Must follow standard email format (`name@domain.com`). Entering an invalid format triggers an alert: "Alternative Email 1 is malformed or contains an invalid domain."

Tab 2 — Eligibility

Field-by-Field Instructions

Click 2. Eligibility on the navigation sidebar. Enter your official Career Executive Service (CES) rank, Educational Management Test (EMT) results, and Civil Service eligibilities.

1. CES Eligibility / Rank Status Optional
What this means
Your Career Executive Service examination stage or presidential conferment rank.
How to select
Click the CES Eligibility / Rank Status dropdown menu and select your status:
  • `Stage 1 (CES Written Examination)`
  • `Stage 2 (Assessment Center)`
  • `Stage 3 (Performance Validation)`
  • `Stage 4 (Board Interview)`
  • `CES Eligible`
  • `CESO Rank I` through `CESO Rank VI`
  • `Not Applicable`
2. Date of CES Conferment Optional
What this means
The official date when your CES eligibility or CESO rank was conferred by the CES Board.
How to select
Click the date field to select the conferment date (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Educational Management Test (EMT) Status & Date Optional
What this means
Indicates whether you passed the DepEd Educational Management Test required for executive positions.
How to select
Under "Are you an EMT Passer?", click the Yes or No button. If you click Yes, the Date passed EMT date picker field will appear below it. Click the picker to select the date you passed the examination.
4. Other Civil Service Eligibility Dynamic List
What this means
A list of additional civil service examinations passed (e.g., Career Service Professional, RA 1080 Board Examinations).
How to add an entry
Click the + Add Eligibility button. A new entry box will appear with four fields:
  • Eligibility: Type the examination name (e.g., CAREER SERVICE PROFESSIONAL).
  • Date of Examination / Conferment: Select the exam date.
  • Rating: Type your examination rating percentage (e.g., 85.50).
  • Place of Examination: Type the exam location (e.g., MANILA).
How to remove an entry
To delete an eligibility entry, hover over the entry box and click the red trash icon in the top right corner.

Tab 3 — Experience

Field-by-Field Instructions & Managerial Experience Calculation

Click 3. Experience on the navigation menu. Enter your employment history and previous positions held.

💡 Automatic Managerial Experience Calculation: The system automatically calculates your total managerial experience (in years and months) from the date ranges of all listed previous positions. You do not need to calculate it manually!
1. Total Managerial Experience Display Auto-Computed
What this means
Displays your total accumulated managerial experience duration (e.g., "5 Years, 3 Months").
Behavior
Updates automatically as you add, edit, or adjust position dates.
2. Career Progression Timeline System Information
What this means
A vertical timeline on the right side of the screen displaying your historical career path and past incumbent records retrieved from system databases.
3. Previous Positions Held List At Least 1 Entry Recommended
How to add a position
Click the + Add Position button. A new position row will appear containing:
  • Position: Select the position title from the dropdown, or select `Others` and type the title.
  • Office / Division: Type the name of the office, division, or station (e.g., SDO QUEZON CITY).
  • From Date: Click the date picker and select the start date of the position.
  • To Date: Click the date picker and select the end date of the position.
Date Range Validation Rule
The To Date must be strictly after the From Date. Selecting an invalid end date displays an error message: "In Managerial Experience #N: To Date must be after From Date."
How to Add an OIC Designation
If you served as an Officer-in-Charge during that position, click the + Add OIC button inside that position box. Enter the OIC Position, OIC Office, and OIC From/To dates.
How to remove a position
Click the yellow trash icon button on the far right of the position row to remove it.

Tab 4 — Education

Field-by-Field Instructions & Degree Sequence Rules

Click 4. Education on the navigation menu. Enter your academic degrees and educational qualifications.

1. Baccalaureate / Bachelor's Degree Required
What to enter
In the Bachelor's Degree box, type your undergraduate degree title (e.g., BACHELOR OF SCIENCE IN SECONDARY EDUCATION). In the Year box, select your 4-digit graduation year.
2. Master's Degree Optional
What to enter
Type your Master's degree title (e.g., MASTER OF ARTS IN EDUCATIONAL MANAGEMENT) and select your graduation year.
Sequence Validation Rule
The Master's graduation year must be strictly greater than your Bachelor's graduation year. Selecting an earlier year triggers a red error popup: "Master's Degree year must be strictly greater than Bachelor's Degree year."
3. Doctorate Degree Optional
What to enter
Type your Doctorate degree title (e.g., DOCTOR OF PHILOSOPHY IN EDUCATION) and select your graduation year.
Sequence Validation Rule
The Doctorate graduation year must be strictly greater than your Master's graduation year. Selecting an earlier year triggers an alert: "Doctorate year must be strictly greater than Master's Degree year."
4. Graduation Year Validation System Rule
Year Rule
All graduation years must be 4-digit numbers from year 1900 to the current year. Entering a year before 1900 triggers an error popup: "Year Graduated must be 1900 or later."

Tab 5 — Performance Ratings

Field-by-Field Instructions & Rating Period Rules

Click 5. Performance Ratings on the navigation menu. Enter your official Office Performance Commitment and Review Form (OPCRF) ratings and CESPES scores.

1. OPCRF Performance Ratings (1st, 2nd, 3rd) Latest Rating Required
Latest Rating (1st)
Type your numerical rating score (e.g., 4.850) in the Latest Rating (1st) box. Click the Period box to select the rating period month and year (Format: YYYY-MM).
Previous Rating (2nd)
Type your second most recent numerical rating and select its period.
Oldest Rating (3rd)
Type your third most recent numerical rating and select its period.
2. CESPES Semester Ratings Optional
CESPES 1st & 2nd Semesters
Enter your 1st Semester and 2nd Semester Career Executive Service Performance Evaluation System (CESPES) ratings and select their respective period months.
⚠️ Rating Period Validation Rules:
  • Rating periods cannot be in the future. (Alert: "Latest Performance Rating period cannot be in the future.")
  • Previous ratings must be older than latest ratings. Selecting an invalid sequence triggers an alert: "Previous Rating period cannot be earlier than Oldest Rating period."

Tab 6 — Professional Development Trainings

Field-by-Field Instructions & Hours Auto-Calculation

Click 6. Prof. Dev. Trainings on the navigation menu. Record all executive training programs, workshops, and seminars attended.

1. Total Number of Training Hours Display Auto-Computed
What this means
Displays your total accumulated training hours (e.g., "120 Hours").
Behavior
Calculates automatically by summing the total hours from every training row added.
2. Training Entry Row Controls At Least 1 Entry Recommended
How to add a training
Click the + Add Training button. A new row will appear with the following fields:
  • Training / Seminar Name: Type the official title of the training program. Converted to UPPERCASE.
  • Date From: Click the date picker and select the training start date.
  • Date To: Click the date picker and select the training end date. Date To must be after Date From.
  • Hrs / Day: Click the dropdown menu and select `8 hrs`, `4 hrs`, or `2 hrs`. The system automatically calculates total hours by multiplying the number of training days by the selected hours per day!
  • Total Hours: Displays the calculated hours (or type a custom total from 0 to 999).
How to remove a training
Click the yellow trash icon button on the right side of the row to remove the entry.

Tab 8 — Documents

Field-by-Field Document Upload Instructions

Click 8. Documents on the navigation menu. Upload mandatory supporting personnel documents to complete your official record file.

Document Title Accepted Formats File Requirements Available Actions
Personal Data Sheet (PDS) `.pdf`, `.doc`, `.docx` Must be a complete, signed, and notarized copy of CSC Form 212. Upload Document, View, Download
Service Records `.pdf` Certified true copy of official service record document. Upload Document, View, Download
2x2 ID Picture `image/*` (`.png`, `.jpg`) Passport-size photo with white background, name tag & signature. Auto-compressed. Upload Document, View, Download

Complete Upload, View, and Download Procedure

  1. To Upload a File: Click the dashed Upload Document button inside the document box. In the file window that opens on your computer, locate and select your document file, then click Open. Wait a few seconds while the system displays "Processing...". Once saved, a green checkmark notice appears stating "Saved ✓".
  2. To View an Uploaded Document: Click the View button. The document will open in a new browser tab for your inspection.
  3. To Download a Copy: Click the Download button. The system will download a copy directly to your computer with an official filename (e.g., `JUAN_DELA_CRUZ_pds.pdf`).
  4. To Replace a File: If you uploaded the wrong document, simply click Upload Document again and select the correct file. The system will automatically overwrite the old file.

Tab 10 — Summary & Certification

Step 1 — Reviewing Profile Data & Exporting Documents

Click 10. Summary & Certify on the navigation menu. Review your consolidated profile data across all sections.

What You See

The consolidated Profile Summary card displaying your personal information, eligibility ranks, educational degrees, performance history ratings, document upload statuses, and legal answers.

What To Do (Exporting Records)

To export a copy of your completed profile, click the dark blue Export Profile button in the top right of the card. Select one of the available export formats:

  • Data Export (CSV): Downloads a `.csv` data spreadsheet.
  • Document (PDF): Generates a printable landscape `.pdf` document via `html2pdf.js`.
  • Presentation (PowerPoint): Generates a 16:9 `.pptx` presentation deck via `PptxGenJS`.

What You Should See Next

Your computer will automatically download the exported file in your chosen format.

Step 2 — Data Privacy Consent & Profile Certification Submission

Scroll down to the bottom of Tab 10 to locate the Data Privacy Notice and Certification section.

What You See

The Data Privacy Notice card (referencing Republic Act No. 10173 - Data Privacy Act of 2012) containing two checkable consent boxes and a Submit Profile / Certify button.

What To Do
  1. Read the Data Privacy Notice statement.
  2. Click Box 1: "I have read and fully understood the Data Privacy Notice above. I hereby give my informed consent..." to place a checkmark in the box.
  3. Click Box 2: "I hereby certify that all information entered in this profile is true, correct, and complete..." to place a checkmark in the box.
  4. Click the Submit Profile / Certify button.
What You Should See Next

Your profile status in the top banner changes to Profiling Completed / In Review, and your submission timestamp (`dpa_consented_at`) is officially recorded in the system.

Important Warning

The Submit Profile / Certify button remains disabled until BOTH consent checkboxes have been checked!

Saving, Editing & Error Reference

Step 1 — Explanation of Save vs. Submit

It is important for all staff to understand the difference between saving a draft and submitting your profile.

Action Button System Behavior Can You Edit Afterwards?
Save Profile
(Sticky Footer Bar)		 Saves your currently entered information as a draft in the database. Displays a "Saving..." loading spinner followed by a temporary green "Saved ✓" notice. YES. You can continue editing your information anytime.
Edit Profile / Cancel Edit
(Header Banner)		 Toggles the form between read-only view mode and editable mode. Prompts a confirmation popup: "Are you sure you want to edit profile info?" Click Yes, edit profile to proceed. YES. Allows you to modify existing data.
Submit Profile / Certify
(Tab 10 Bottom)		 Officially certifies your record, records your Data Privacy consent timestamp, and updates your profile status to Profiling Completed or In Review for Personnel Division evaluation. FINAL SUBMISSION. Requires administrative unlock if revisions are needed after review.

Step 2 — Validation Error Message Troubleshooting Table

If the system displays a popup alert when you click Save or Submit, locate the exact message below to fix it:

Exact Popup Alert Wording Cause of Error Correction Step
"Date of Birth cannot be in the future." Selected birth date is today or a future date. Go to Tab 1 and select your correct past birth date.
"Please specify your Position Title." Position Title is set to `Others` but the custom text box is empty. Go to Tab 1 and type your position title in the text box.
"Please specify your Designation." OIC toggle is ON but Designation is empty or `Others` without text. Go to Tab 1 and select or type your OIC designation.
"Alternative Email 1 is malformed..." Email address format is invalid (missing @ or domain). Go to Tab 1 and enter a valid email (e.g., name@gmail.com).
"Year Graduated must be 1900 or later." Graduation year entered is before 1900. Go to Tab 4 and select a valid 4-digit graduation year.
"Master's Degree year must be strictly greater..." Master's graduation year is equal to or earlier than Bachelor's year. Go to Tab 4 and correct the graduation years to be chronological.
"Latest Performance Rating period cannot be in the future." Rating period selected is after current month. Go to Tab 5 and select a past or current rating period.
"In Managerial Experience #N: To Date must be after From Date." End date is earlier than start date for a position. Go to Tab 3 and adjust the From and To dates.
